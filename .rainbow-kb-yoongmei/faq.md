# Yoong Mei — Frequently Asked Questions

## About Yoong Mei

**Q: What does Yoong Mei do?**
A: Yoong Mei Trading And Transport Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian logistics company providing
LCL and FCL freight transport services, primarily from Nilai (Negeri Sembilan) to major
destinations across Peninsular Malaysia, plus cross-border services to Singapore and Thailand.

**Q: Where is Yoong Mei based?**
A: Our operations origin is Nilai, Negeri Sembilan.

**Q: How do I contact Yoong Mei?**
A: Website: http://www.yoongmei.com | Enquiry form: https://yoongmei.com/contact | Email: info@yoongmei.net

## Transport Services

**Q: What is LCL vs FCL?**
A: LCL (Less than Container Load) — you pay per pallet; cargo is consolidated with others.
FCL (Full Container Load) — you charter the entire lorry; best for large volumes.

**Q: What lorry sizes do you have?**
A: We operate 8-ton lorries, 32-footers (12 tons), and 40-footers (22/24/25/28/30 tons).

**Q: Do you do cross-border?**
A: Yes — we serve both Singapore and Thailand. Contact us for a customised quote.

**Q: What destinations do you cover?**
A: From Nilai: Johor Bahru, Melaka, Selangor, Rawang, Ipoh, Penang Mainland, Penang Island,
Singapore, and Thailand. International: China (Yiwu, Guangzhou, Humen), Vietnam (Hanoi),
Indonesia (Jakarta), Thailand (Bangkok, Sadao).

**Q: Can you pick up from locations other than Nilai? / Boleh pickup dari tempat lain?**
A: Our listed rates are for shipments originating from Nilai. However, we have branches in
Puchong (+60 16-723 9206), Klang (+60 16-723 9206), Bukit Mertajam (+60 16-723 9207),
and Kuantan (+60 16-629 3200). For pickups from other locations (e.g., Shah Alam, KL,
Subang), contact our nearest branch to arrange collection. Our team can provide a custom
quote for non-Nilai origin pickups.

**Q: Do you offer import services from China? / 从中国进口**
A: Yes. We offer consoled import, FCL import, and LCL import services from China. Our China
warehouses are in Yiwu (Zhejiang), Guangzhou Tianhe, and Humen (Guangdong). For import
enquiries including pickup from your Chinese supplier, customs declaration, and delivery to
Malaysia, contact our team at info@yoongmei.net with your shipment details.

## Pricing & Quoting

**Q: How do I get a price?**
A: You can ask here for an estimated rate based on your destination and shipment type.
For a confirmed quote, provide your full shipment details and our team will revert.

**Q: What information do I need to provide for a quote?**
A: We need:
1. Your name
2. Pick-up name & address
3. Delivery name & address
4. Commodity (what goods)
5. Quantity
6. Weight
7. Dimensions
8. Full billing company details

**Q: Are the rates on this chat final?**
A: No — the rates shared here are indicative/estimated only. Our team will confirm the
final rate based on your specific shipment.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for large volumes? / Ada diskaun untuk kuantiti besar?**
A: Our pallet rates are already tiered — the more pallets you ship, the lower the per-pallet
rate (e.g., 5+ pallets get a better rate than 2+ pallets). For volumes beyond our listed
tiers (e.g., 20+ pallets or regular/recurring shipments), our team can discuss custom
pricing. Contact info@yoongmei.net with your shipment details and volume for a tailored quote.

**Q: How do I get a quote for international shipping from China?**
A: For imports from China (Yiwu, Guangzhou, Humen), please provide: origin city/warehouse
in China, commodity type, quantity/weight/dimensions, and delivery address in Malaysia.
Our team will provide a custom quote covering pickup, shipping, customs declaration, and
last-mile delivery. Email info@yoongmei.net or submit via https://yoongmei.com/contact.

## Operations

**Q: What commodities do you handle?**
A: We handle general cargo and industrial goods. For hazmat, perishables, or special
requirements, please contact our team directly for assessment.

**Q: How long does delivery take? / Berapa hari penghantaran?**
A: Typical transit times from Nilai (anggaran masa penghantaran dari Nilai):
- Johor Bahru: 1–2 working days
- Melaka: 1 working day
- Selangor / Rawang: 1 working day
- Ipoh / Penang / Pulau Pinang: 2–3 working days
- Cross-border (Singapore / Thailand): contact team for specific lead time.
These are estimates — actual times depend on schedule and service type.

**Q: Is there an urgent or express service?**
A: We do not have a fixed express tier, but you can contact our team at info@yoongmei.net
to enquire about priority scheduling depending on route availability.

**Q: Can I track my shipment?**
A: Please contact our team at info@yoongmei.net for shipment tracking information.

**Q: What is a Console service?**
A: Console is a shared lorry service on a fixed scheduled run. It's more economical than full charter
for medium cargo volumes. Available in 20ft and 30ft options. Cargo from multiple customers is
consolidated into one lorry.

**Q: What's the minimum shipment you accept?**
A: For LCL pallet service, you can ship as little as 1 pallet. For full charter, you charter the
entire lorry regardless of how much cargo you have. Contact us if you have a very small shipment
and we'll recommend the right service.

**Q: Do you handle hazardous goods?**
A: Hazardous materials (hazmat), flammable goods, and perishables may require special assessment.
Please contact our team directly at info@yoongmei.net to discuss your specific requirements.

**Q: What are your payment terms?**
A: Payment terms are discussed and agreed upon with our team as part of the confirmed quotation.
Please ask our team when they revert with your quote.

**Q: Can I make changes or cancel a confirmed shipment?**
A: Please contact our team directly at info@yoongmei.net or https://yoongmei.com/contact as soon
as possible. Changes to confirmed shipments are subject to team approval depending on timing.

**Q: Do you provide packing services?**
A: Yes, packing services are available as part of our warehousing solutions. We also offer
stuffing & unstuffing services. For transport-only shipments, cargo should be properly
packed and palletised by the shipper before collection.

Saturday: 8:30am – 1:00pm (MYT)
Closed on Sundays and Malaysian public holidays.
