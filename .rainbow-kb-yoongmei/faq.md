## Services

**Q: What transport services do you offer?**
A: We offer: cross-border lorry transport (Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Laos, China), Peninsular Malaysia inland delivery (FTL and LTL), warehousing and distribution, customs clearance and documentation, and international sea freight (LCL and FCL).

**Q: Do you ship to Singapore?**
A: Yes. We operate regular cross-border haulage between Johor Bahru and Singapore, including customs clearance on both sides.

**Q: Do you have a registered entity in Singapore?**
A: Yes. We have a Singapore-incorporated company — YOONG MEI TRADING & TRANSPORT PTE. LTD. (UEN: 202020814W), registered at 527 Woodlands Drive 14 #03-503, Woodlands Vista, Singapore 730527. For Singapore cross-border enquiries, contact info@yoongmei.net or +60 16-723 9207.

**Q: Do you ship to Thailand?**
A: Yes. We cover routes up to the Bukit Kayu Hitam / Hat Yai corridor at the Malaysia-Thailand border.

**Q: Do you handle sea freight to China?**
A: Yes. We offer LCL and FCL import services from China, as well as Vietnam, Indonesia, and Thailand.

**Q: What types of cargo do you handle?**
A: We handle most types of general industrial goods including: chemicals, electrical appliances, cables, wire rods, plastic resins, metal plates, plastic molds, brass rods, galvanized pipes, extrusion pipes, and general commercial cargo.

**Q: Do you offer warehousing?**
A: Yes. We provide general cargo storage, stuffing & unstuffing, 3PL/4PL logistics, distribution, and packing services at our Masai (JB), Puchong, and Klang facilities.

**Q: Can you handle customs clearance?**
A: Yes. We provide full import/export customs declaration, Singapore GST permit handling, port clearance documentation, and customs consultancy services.

**Q: Do you cover all of Peninsular Malaysia?**
A: Yes. With branches in Johor Bahru, Selangor (Puchong and Klang), Penang, and Pahang, we provide delivery coverage across all major states in Peninsular Malaysia.

## Pricing & Quotations

**Q: How much does freight cost?**
A: All our rates are quote-based — pricing depends on the route, cargo type, weight/volume, and service requirements. Contact us at info@yoongmei.net or +60 16-723 9207 for a quotation.

**Q: How do I get a freight quote?**
A: Share the following details with us and we'll provide a fast quote:
1. Origin and destination
2. Cargo description (type of goods)
3. Weight and dimensions (kg / CBM)
4. Required pickup or delivery date
5. Any special requirements

Email info@yoongmei.net or call +60 16-723 9207.

**Q: Is there a minimum shipment size?**
A: We handle both FTL (full truckload) and LTL (less than truckload) shipments, as well as LCL sea freight — so there is no strict minimum. Contact us with your cargo details for guidance.
