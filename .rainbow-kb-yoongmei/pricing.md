# Pricing — Yoong Mei Trading And Transport Sdn Bhd

> All rates are customised based on route, cargo type, volume, and service requirements.

---

## How Pricing Works

Yoong Mei does **not** publish fixed rate cards. All freight rates are:

- **Quote-based** — tailored to your specific shipment
- Dependent on: origin, destination, cargo type, cargo weight/volume, urgency, and service level required
- Confirmed by the operations team before booking

---

## Factors That Affect Your Rate

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Origin & destination | Route distance and cross-border fees |
| Cargo type | Hazardous goods, oversized loads, or specialised handling may incur additional charges |
| Weight & volume | CBM (cubic metres) and tonnage pricing for sea freight |
| Container mode | FCL vs LCL — FCL is more cost-effective for large volumes |
| Customs requirements | Declaration fees, GST permits (for Singapore), port clearance |
| Warehousing duration | Storage fees for extended periods |
| Urgency | Express or priority delivery options |
