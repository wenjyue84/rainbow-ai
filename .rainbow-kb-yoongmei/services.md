# Services — Yoong Mei Trading And Transport Sdn Bhd

> Freight, logistics, warehousing, and customs clearance services across Malaysia and Southeast Asia.

---

## 1. Cross-Border Lorry Transport

Commercial lorry transportation across Malaysia and Southeast Asia.

**Routes covered:**
- Malaysia (nationwide, all major industrial zones)
- Singapore (cross-border haulage from Johor Bahru)
- Thailand (up to Bukit Kayu Hitam / Hat Yai corridor)
- Vietnam, Laos, China (international haulage routes)

**Local & outstation trips:**
- Local deliveries within branch areas (JB, Selangor, Penang, Pahang)
- Outstation trips: JB ↔ Bukit Kayu Hitam (Thai border), JB ↔ Singapore

---

## 2. Warehousing & Distribution

General warehousing with value-added logistics services.

- General cargo storage
- Stuffing & unstuffing (container packing/unpacking)
- 3PL (Third-Party Logistics)
- 4PL (Fourth-Party Logistics)
- Distribution services
- Packing & repacking services

**Warehouse locations:** Masai (JB HQ), Puchong (Selangor), Klang (Selangor)

---

## 3. Customs Clearance & Documentation

Full customs brokerage and forwarding services.

- Import & export customs declaration
- Singapore GST permit handling
- Port clearance documentation
- Forwarding & documentation services
- Customs consultancy
- Regulatory compliance advice

---

## 4. International Shipping

Sea freight for import/export cargo.

- **LCL (Less than Container Load)** import services
- **FCL (Full Container Load)** import services
- **Consolidated shipments** for smaller cargo volumes

**Countries serviced:**
- China
- Indonesia
- Thailand
- Vietnam

---

## 5. Peninsular Malaysia Inland Delivery

- FTL (Full Truckload) delivery
- LTL (Less than Truckload) delivery
- Coverage across all major states in Peninsular Malaysia

---

## Types of Goods Handled

Yoong Mei specialises in industrial and commercial freight, including:

- Chemicals
- Electrical appliances
- Cables and wire rods
- Plastic resins and plastic molds
- Metal plates and brass rods
- Galvanized pipes and extrusion pipes
- General industrial cargo
