# Parking

## Availability
- **On-site parking:** Limited — please contact us in advance if you need a guaranteed spot.
- **Off-site public parking:** Available nearby at street-level public bays.

## Cost
- **Public street parking:** RM 0.60 per hour (~US$ 0.15 / hour).
- Coupons/app payment via JB City Council parking system during working hours (weekdays 07:30–17:30, Sat 07:30–13:00).
- **Outside working hours, Sundays, and public holidays:** Free.

## Tips for Guests
- Street parking spots around Jalan Pelangi fill up during weekends and public holidays — arrive early or use nearby paid carparks at KSL City Mall / Plaza Pelangi as overflow (short walk).
- If driving in after 10pm, message us so we can advise on the safest overnight spots nearby.
- We do **not** charge guests for parking — payment is directly to the JB City Council meters/app.

## Motorbikes & Bicycles
- Motorbike parking available along the front of the building (free).
- Bicycle storage can be arranged inside — just ask at check-in.
