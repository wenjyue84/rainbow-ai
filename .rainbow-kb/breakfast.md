# Breakfast & Meals

## Is Breakfast Included?
**No — breakfast is NOT included in the room rate.** Pelangi Capsule Hostel does not serve breakfast.

## What We Offer Instead
- **Fully-equipped shared kitchen** (free to use, 24/7) — microwave, kettle, cookware, utensils, hot/cold water dispenser, coffee machine. You can cook or prepare your own meals.
- **Coffee machine** available all day in the common area.

## Eating Out Nearby
The hostel is in **Taman Pelangi**, surrounded by dozens of affordable local restaurants, kopitiam (Chinese-style coffee shops), and mamak stalls.

- **Typical Malaysian breakfast** — nasi lemak or roti canai, costs RM 3–8 at nearby eateries.
- **Walking distance** (< 5 min): several kopitiam and mamak stalls open from ~6am.
- **Popular pick:** Best roti canai spots in the area — see the blog guide for recommendations.

## Quick Answer
- No breakfast served by the hostel.
- Use the shared kitchen to cook, OR walk 2–5 minutes to local eateries (RM 3–8 typical).
