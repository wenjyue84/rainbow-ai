# Pre-Order Flow

Makan Moments Cafe offers online pre-ordering so customers can have their food ready when they arrive.

## How It Works

1. **Browse the Menu** — Visit our website to see the full menu with photos and prices
2. **Add to Tray** — Select dishes and add them to your tray (like a virtual order)
3. **Submit Order** — Provide your Malaysian phone number (01X-XXXXXXX) and estimated arrival time (minimum 15 minutes from now)
4. **Admin Approves** — Our team reviews and approves your order
5. **Pay Deposit** — After approval, pay a Touch 'n Go deposit by scanning the QR code and uploading the screenshot
6. **Food Prepared** — Our kitchen starts preparing your order before you arrive
7. **Arrive & Enjoy** — Come to the cafe and your food is ready!

## Important Notes

- **Minimum lead time:** 15 minutes before arrival
- **Deposit:** Via Touch 'n Go (TNG) e-wallet — scan QR, upload screenshot
- **Final payment:** At the cafe counter (cash or e-wallet via FeedMe POS)
- **Order tracking:** You'll receive a short order ID to check your order status anytime
- **Cancellation:** Contact us at 012-708 8789 if you need to cancel

## Order Status Guide

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| Pending Approval | Your order was submitted and is awaiting our team's review |
| Approved | Order accepted! Please pay the TNG deposit |
| Payment Uploaded | We received your deposit screenshot |
| Preparing | Kitchen is cooking your order |
| Ready | Your food is ready for pickup! |
| Rejected | Order was not accepted (a reason will be provided) |
