# Southern Homestay — Staff Management Guide

## Property Overview
- **Type:** Homestay / rooms
- **Management:** Separate deployment from Pelangi Capsule
- **Check-in:** 3:00 PM | **Check-out:** 12:00 PM (noon)

## Daily Operations

### Check-in Procedure
1. Verify guest identity (passport or IC)
2. Confirm booking in PMS2
3. Assign room
4. Collect payment if not prepaid
5. Provide room key and access instructions
6. Show guest around the property
7. Record check-in in PMS2

### Check-out Procedure
1. Retrieve room key from guest
2. Inspect room for damages
3. Process any outstanding charges
4. Mark checkout in PMS2
5. Prepare room for next guest

## Common Issues

### Maintenance Requests
- Log issue in PMS2 under the room
- Tag maintenance status on the unit
- Contact repair team if urgent

### Guest Complaints
- Listen, acknowledge, apologize
- Resolve immediately where possible
- Escalate to Jay for unresolved issues
- Log complaint in PMS2 notes

## Emergency Procedures
- Fire: evacuate, call 999
- Medical: call 999, notify Jay immediately
- Theft: secure scene, call police (999), notify Jay
