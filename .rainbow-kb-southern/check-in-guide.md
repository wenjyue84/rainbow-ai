## KSL D'Esplanade Residence

### Block A (with car)
1. Park in the Visitor area (outdoor) for up to 20 minutes
2. Head to the Guard house, walk ~30 seconds, Block A is on your right
3. Enter through glass door, turn left — mailbox is straight ahead on the left
4. After getting room card: park at any spot on Level 3/4, Block A side
5. Display visitor pass on dashboard to avoid RM200 clamp fee

### Block B (with car)
1. Park in the Visitor area (outdoor) for up to 20 minutes
2. Head to the Guard house, walk ~30 seconds, Block B is on your left
3. Enter through glass door, turn left — mailbox is straight ahead on the left
4. Video guide: https://youtu.be/_kzKZA037ow
5. After getting room card: park at Level 3/4, Block B side
6. Display visitor pass on dashboard to avoid RM200 clamp fee

### KSL Facilities
- Pool & Gym: Level 7, 8 AM to 7 PM

### Entering the Unit
Touch the screen pad by the front door or press any nearby button, then scan the provided card. The door will open automatically.

### Check-Out Procedure
Place the access card on the unit dining table, take a photo as proof of check-out.

## Setia Sky88 Residence

### Card Collection
Collect your room card at **Apam Balik food stall** in Food Court myWarung (opposite the condominium).
- Address: 18, Jalan Dato Abdullah Tahir, Taman Abad, 80400 Johor Bahru, Johor
- Look for the stall named "Apam Balik Jalil" or "Goreng Pisang & Goreng Keropok"
- Tell your house number, staff will check and pass the room card

- Video guide: https://youtu.be/o0lTHaC-beM
