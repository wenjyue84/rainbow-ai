## Cancellation & Modification Policy

Last-minute changes (within 2 days of check-in):
- **Modify booking:** RM50 fee
- **Cancel booking:** RM100 fee (deposit forfeited)

### Cancelling on Airbnb
1. Log in to Airbnb
2. Go to "Trips" or "Reservations"
3. Find the booking, open details
4. Check cancellation policy
5. Click "Cancel Reservation"
6. Select reason, review refund policy
7. Confirm and save confirmation
