> Common questions about Southern Homestay.

## About Southern Homestay

**Q: What is Southern Homestay?**
A: Southern Homestay Group is a property management company based in Johor Bahru, Malaysia. We manage over 50 homestays across KSL D'Esplanade Residence, Setia Sky88, and Pinnacle Towers — offering clean, comfortable apartment stays for short-term guests.

**Q: Where is Southern Homestay located?**
A: Our properties are in Johor Bahru:
- **KSL D'Esplanade Residence** — next to KSL City Mall
- **Setia Sky88** — Jalan Dato Abdullah Tahir, 80300 Johor Bahru
- **Pinnacle Towers** — central Johor Bahru

**Q: Who runs Southern Homestay?**
A: Southern Homestay is operated by Jay (Wenjyue Lew). The registered business name is Rumah Tumpangan Selatan Lima Bintang.

## Booking & Pricing

**Q: How much does it cost per night?**
A: Rates vary by property and room type:
- KSL Studio: from RM180/night (weekday), RM230/night (weekend)
- Sky88 2-Bedroom: from RM165/night (weekday), RM215/night (weekend)
Weekend = Friday and Saturday nights. Contact us for exact pricing and availability.

**Q: How do I book?**
A: You can book through:
- **Airbnb** — https://www.airbnb.com/users/show/187216391
- **Booking.com** — search "Southern Homestay Johor Bahru"
- **WhatsApp** — contact us directly for private bookings

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Bank transfer to our CIMB account. Please include your unit number and booking name in the transfer note (e.g., KSLB2011-Lily). Share the bank-in slip for verification.

**Q: Is a deposit required?**
A: Yes, a safety deposit is required before check-in to protect the property. The amount depends on your booking. Transfer to our CIMB account and share the bank slip.

## Check-In & Check-Out

**Q: What time is check-in?**
A: Check-in is at 4:00 PM. We'll send you the mailbox code or card details at 4 PM (or earlier if the house is ready).

**Q: What time is check-out?**
A: Check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Place the access card on the dining table and take a photo as proof.

**Q: Can I check in early or check out late?**
A: Yes, subject to availability and extra fees (e.g., Studio: RM25/hour, 2BR: RM30/hour). After 3 PM on check-out day counts as an extra night. Contact us to arrange.

**Q: How do I collect my room card at Sky88?**
A: Collect at the Apam Balik food stall in Food Court myWarung (opposite Sky88). Available from 4 PM to 11 PM. Tell them your unit number.

**Q: How do I enter my KSL unit?**
A: Touch the screen pad by the front door or press a nearby button, then scan the provided card. The door opens automatically.

## Facilities & Amenities

**Q: Is there parking?**
A: Yes. At KSL, park at Level 3/4 and display the visitor pass on your dashboard (RM200 clamp fee if not displayed). At Sky88, park at Level 4 only.

**Q: Is there a pool or gym?**
A: At KSL — Pool & Gym on Level 7, open 8 AM to 7 PM.

**Q: Do you have Netflix?**
A: We don't provide Netflix pre-installed. If your unit has a Smart TV, you can install it yourself. Smart TVs support YouTube; Digital TVs only have local channels.

**Q: Is there WiFi?**
A: Yes, WiFi is available in all units. Details will be provided at check-in.

## Cancellation & Changes

**Q: Can I cancel my booking?**
A: Last-minute changes within 2 days of check-in:
- Modify booking: RM50 fee
- Cancel booking: RM100 fee (deposit forfeited)
For Airbnb/Booking.com bookings, follow their cancellation process.
