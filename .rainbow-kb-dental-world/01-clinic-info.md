## General aftercare guidance (non-diagnostic)

- After extraction: bite on the gauze for 30–45 minutes, avoid hot drinks, avoid rinsing
  vigorously for 24 hours, no smoking, take prescribed painkillers as directed.
- After scaling: mild sensitivity for a day or two is normal.
- After whitening: avoid strongly coloured food/drink (coffee, tea, curry) for 48 hours.
- Toothache first aid: rinse with warm salt water, use a common painkiller such as
  paracetamol as directed, and see a dentist promptly.

## Emergencies

Severe facial swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or facial
trauma: go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.
