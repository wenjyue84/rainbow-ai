## Iteration 8 - Story: Validate Conversation Message Timestamp Ordering with Clock Skew Detection (US-468)

1. **Core Module: src/assistant/pipeline/timestamp-validator.ts** (125 lines)
   - `validateTimestamps(messages, conversationId)`: validates ChatMessage[] timestamp ordering

     null timestamps, empty conversations, max_gap_ms, default conversationId

**3. Recommended Implementation Approach:**
